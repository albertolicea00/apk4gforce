# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

**4GForce** is a free, open-source Android application developed by @albertolicea00.

## Data Collection

4GForce does **not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**. The app operates entirely on your device and does not connect to the internet.

## Permissions

4GForce requires **zero permissions**. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device feature.

## What the App Does

The app opens Android's built-in radio settings menu so you can manually change your preferred network type. It does not modify any device settings on its own.

## Third-Party Services

4GForce does not use any third-party services, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
